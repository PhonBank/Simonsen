### Project Description

Two children, a girl, Nora (2;3 to 3;5) and a boy, Tomas (2;0 to 4;1)
were recorded in connection with a doctoral project on child phonology
over the years 1985-1989. Both children were monolingual speakers of the
Oslo dialect of Norwegian, as were their parents. Nora was recorded over
6 sessions between 2;3 and 3;5, while Tomas was recorded over 7 sessions
between 2;0 and 4;1. Both children were firstborns, with younger
siblings born during the period of recording. The children were recorded
in their homes in Oslo in a naturalistic setting, always with the
researcher and often also with the mother present. During the recording
sessions the children looked at picture books and played with toys. The
books and toys were chosen to elicit words to represent all the
Norwegian speech sounds in different positions and contexts. The
researcher encouraged spontaneous word productions from the child, often
through pointing to pictures and toys and repeating the child’s words.

The recordings were made using an analog tape recorder with a
multidirectional microphone placed on the table. The tapes were later
digitized and finally transferred into an MP3 format.
